# Speaker Notes - AI-SDLC Webinar (30 min)

Open this file on a second monitor during the presentation. Press **H** to toggle the on-screen timer/HUD.

---

## Slide 1: AI-SDLC at Vesta
- **Navigation hint:** Use arrow keys or buttons. Press H for timer.
- Set the tone: speed + quality, not speed OR quality.

---

## Slide 2: Why AI-SDLC exists
- **Prompt:** "Where do we lose most time after code is written?" → Then move to next slide to reveal the flow.
- Key insight: coding is cheap; alignment, review, and verification are the new bottleneck.

---

## Slide 3: Mature systems fail through slow entropy
- **Interactive:** Click an example in the grid. Ask: "Would code review catch this reliably?"
- Call out: entropy examples are subtle—AI can help flag them, but only with context.

---

## Slide 4: What AI-SDLC is (and isn't)
- Emphasize: this is a method, not a tool rollout.
- Quote: "Human accountability is the loss function."

---

## Slide 5: Why it matters to Vesta
- Tie to AI-First strategy: "AI-SDLC in at least one product per BU."
- Mention baseline expectations within ~90 days.

---

## Slide 6: Lifecycle + gates
- **Interactive:** Click phases to show mandatory outputs.
- Use this as shared language across technical and non-technical roles.

---

## Slide 7: Non-negotiables
- **Tip:** Keep returning to three anchors: plan-first, small batches, proof over prose.
- Progressive reveal builds to the gatekeeper drill.

---

## Slide 8: Artifacts + traceability
- Emphasize: artifacts are durable context for humans and AI. Chat history is not an audit trail.
- Show the repo structure example.

---

## Slide 9: Construction loop + rails
- **Prompt:** "What would you need to see to merge an AI-generated PR with confidence?"
- Walk through the construction loop: plan → design → implement → verify → report.

---

## Slide 10: Brownfield + frontend
- **Tip:** Call out: most BU value is in brownfield, so this is where AI-SDLC either earns trust or fails.
- Discovery is mandatory before inception.

---

## Slide 11: Adoption into BAU
- **Audience callout:** Non-technical colleagues are critical for intent, constraints, acceptance criteria, and gatekeeping.
- Walk through 30/60/90 timeline.

---

## Slide 12: Workshops + resources
- Point to explainer site and SharePoint folder.
- "If you do one thing: run one real unit end-to-end with evidence, then template what worked."

---

## Slide 13: Close
- **Q&A prompt:** "What is the riskiest part of your SDLC today?" → Map it to a gate or artifact.
- Reinforce the three anchors: plan-first, small batches, proof over prose.
